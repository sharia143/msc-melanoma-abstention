# Licence and data statement

## Code

The code in this repository (`src/`) was written by Sharia Alam for the
MSc Data Science dissertation at Manchester Metropolitan University and is made available
for examination. It may be reused for research and teaching with attribution.

## Data

No image data, labels, trained weights or per-image scores are redistributed here. Each
dataset is governed by its own licence and must be obtained from its source:

- **ISIC-DICM-17K** (Ahammed, Cui, Lu and Yap, CVPR Workshops 2025): metadata from the
  MMU dermatology research group; images from the ISIC Archive under the ISIC licence
  terms (CC BY-NC for most collections).
- **MILK10k** (Tschandl et al., Journal of Investigative Dermatology 2026): from the ISIC
  Archive under its licence (CC BY-NC). Its concept probabilities were generated by
  MONET (Kim et al., Nature Medicine 2024).
- **Derm7pt** (Kawahara, Daneshvar, Argenziano and Hamarneh, IEEE JBHI 2019): from the
  authors' site after accepting their terms of use.
- **DFUC2021** (Yap et al., DFUC 2021, LNCS 13183): by request from the challenge
  organisers at Manchester Metropolitan University under their licence agreement. The
  organisers ask that Yap et al. (2021), Cassidy et al. (2021) and Goyal et al. (2020)
  be cited; the dissertation does so.
- **Oxford-IIIT Pet** (Parkhi, Vedaldi, Zisserman and Jawahar, CVPR 2012): public;
  downloaded through `torchvision`.
